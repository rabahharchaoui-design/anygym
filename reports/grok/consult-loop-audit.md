1. **BLOCKER** — `run-overnight.sh:157` and `:159`. Each `claude` run is started with no `CLAUDE_CODE_PRINT_BG_WAIT_CEILING_MS`. 2. **BLOCKER** — `run-overnight.sh:38-46` and `:146-149`. The pace line is a straight climb from `p0+20` to 85% at 4 hours before Thursday 20:00 Guam. Live `.week-pace` is `1795687200 1795082457 0` (snapshot Thursday Nov 19 20:00, week started at 0, reset Thursday Nov 26 20:00). Span is 590343 seconds, so the line rises 0.396 points an hour. At 03:23 the tap showed week 38 and the line allowed 41, so `WEEK_START_GAP` (4) refused to start. After a run spends that slack, the next 4 points take 10.1 hours. While the line is already on the 85 cap, week 82, 83, or 84 never gets 4 points of room, so the loop sleeps 20 minutes at a time until Thursday 20:00. The phone gets no push for that wait. Smallest fix: keep the 85 cap, drop the 4 point start gap once the slope cannot open it before the reset, and let a run that does start continue until `week >= allowed + 10` (or a minimum of 45 minutes) so startup tokens are not the whole run.

Smallest fix: treat a stale tap as over the limit, ignore a probe that did not refresh `ts=`, always call `hold_set` when `five>=74` even if the week line also tripped, refuse to start above 65, and hard-stop at 83.

4. **MAJOR** — `run-overnight.sh:48`, `:52`, `:176-179`, and `:188-193`. `.five-override` is cleared only after a normal end or a `.usage-stop`.
