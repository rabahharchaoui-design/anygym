- MAJOR `src/lib/gps/stillness.ts:27` — Standing is a 4 m circle, while the filter keeps fixes with an accuracy as poor as 25 m, so GPS wander is counted as running before auto-pause can start. With the repo's own standing wander (accuracy 20 m, sigma 8 m, five minutes, one fix a second) seeds 1–5 add 58.7 m, 106.3 m, 172.6 m, 54.8 m and 52.0 m, and 25 s, 43 s, 54 s, 19 s and 22 s of moving time. The checked test only covers sigma 2 m and accuracy 8 m, which stays under 10 m. A runner who ties a shoe sees that drift as distance. Smallest fix: treat the window as standing when every point lies within max(4 m, those points' accuracy) of the newest, and leave any shorter leg out of the distance.
- MAJOR `src/lib/gps/splits.ts:55` — The resume step after auto-pause is untimed (`src/lib/gps/tracker.ts:126` sets `timed: false` and `gap: false` because resume waits for max(4 m, both accuracy radii)), and `addSplitStep` then marks the split a gap whenever that untimed step adds distance. Run 200 s at 3 m/s, stand 25 s at 5 m accuracy, then run 200 s more: the first kilometer is stored `gap: true` (988 m of the 1000 m have time, the split is 336 s) and the summary replaces the time with "GPS gap" plus "part of that split has no time". The same stop at 15 m accuracy flags the open split. The signal never dropped. SPEC 12.2 reserves that label for a GPS stop. `src/lib/gps/__tests__/splits-pace.test.ts:184` and `src/lib/run/__tests__/live-4.3.test.ts:239` currently require the flag. Smallest fix: set the split's gap only when `step.gap` is true, and keep those untimed meters out of the pace.
- MAJOR `src/hooks/runEngine.ts:103` — Hiding the page only saves. The next fix becomes a gap only when the hole is longer than 15 s. A 12 s lock is stored as an ordinary step: those seconds count as moving time and the route is a solid line. SPEC 12.2 says a lock or an app switch is a dashed gap whose time is not moving time, and the pocket line says locking pauses GPS. If the page survives a longer hole that is still under 5 minutes and under 8 m/s, `src/lib/gps/tracker.ts:113` adds the straight-line meters to the distance. If iOS kills the page, restore pauses and the next fix adds 0 m ("Paused while the app was closed"). One 90 s lock is a straight-line credit or nothing, depending on which way the phone left. Smallest fix: on `visibilityState === 'hidden'`, mark the run so the next fix is a gap of distance 0 whose time is not counted, however short the hole was.
- MAJOR `src/screens/run/RunMapGl.tsx:95` — Every map `error` latches `failed`, idle clears "Map offline" only while that flag is false (`:115`), and the only retry is `window` `online` (`:122`), which reloads the style.
