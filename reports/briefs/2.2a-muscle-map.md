=== BRIEF START ===
AnyGym: Premium Interactive Muscle Map

Rebuild AnyGym's existing muscle map into a premium, anatomically accurate, interactive muscle visualization that works beautifully on iPhone and desktop.

Important: Do not automatically use react-native-body-highlighter as previously planned. Evaluate these alternatives first.

1. Research these five libraries
Option 1 (first choice to evaluate): js-rich-body-highlighter, https://github.com/crmapache/js-rich-body-highlighter, demo https://js-rich-body-highlighter.vercel.app/ (male and female, front and back, detailed illustrations, interactive highlighting, SVG masks with realistic shading, React compatible).
Option 2: react-muscle-highlighter, https://github.com/soroojshehryar/react-muscle-highlighter (React and TypeScript, male and female, front and back, individual muscle selection, custom fills and outlines).
Option 3: @emmorts/body-muscles, https://github.com/emmorts/body-muscles, demo https://emmorts.github.io/body-muscles/ (89 regions, interactive SVG, custom colors and intensity, may need extra work for female).
Option 4: MuscleMap, https://github.com/Jsplice/MuscleMap (male and female, front and back, individual muscle surfaces, fitness heatmaps, check its vector only implementation).
Option 5: Sasha's Body Map, https://github.com/Olkre/Sasha-s-Body-Map (male and female, interactive regions, smooth transitions; design inspiration only unless commercial reuse rights are confirmed).
Also consider react-native-body-highlighter as a fallback.

2. Mandatory male and female models: male front, male back, female front, female back, all with equal visual quality. Simple male/female toggle, smooth front/back transitions, persisted preference, proper proportions for each model, all relevant muscle regions independently selectable, the same exercise highlights anatomically corresponding muscles on both models, no generic male silhouette reused as female. Do not sacrifice female functionality to use a particular library.

3. Premium visual design matching AnyGym: black background, the app's electric lime accent, light text, grey secondary text, dark graphite resting muscles. Smooth professional anatomical illustrations, clearly distinguishable muscle groups, realistic contours and subtle depth, no blocky low poly look, no cartoon anatomy, no stock silhouettes, no AI generated anatomy imagery, subtle shadows and separation, primary muscles electric lime, secondary muscles softer dimmer lime, non targeted muscles graphite, subtle glow only on highlighted muscles, minimal clutter. Premium fitness app, not a bodybuilding website.

4. Interactivity: primary muscles bright, secondary reduced intensity, unused graphite, all from actual exercise data, never arbitrary. Tap a muscle to identify it and show its name; show exercises targeting it only where the exercise database supports it. Highlight transitions about 200 to 300 ms, polished front/back transition, no heavy animation, respect reduced motion, prefer a clean 2D transition over 3D rotation. iPhone first, sharp at 320 px, works on modern iPhones and desktop, no accidental scrolling or clipping, usable touch targets.

5. Technical: React web app, favor compatible libraries. Before implementing: inspect the existing muscle map code; compare the libraries using real demos and source; verify male/female support; verify muscle level interaction and IDs; verify commercial licensing for both code AND artwork (an MIT or Apache repo license does not automatically cover bundled third party anatomy art); assess mobile performance and maintenance; pick the strongest. Preserve license notices and attribution. If none reaches the quality bar, build a custom styled implementation from legally reusable SVG anatomy. Never generate anatomy with AI and never purchase artwork without G's approval.

6. Preserve existing functionality: workout sessions, exercise selection, exercise to muscle mappings, primary/secondary classifications, workout history, analytics, navigation, saved data. Visual and interaction upgrade only; reuse existing infrastructure.

7. QA: test all four views, primary and secondary highlighting, muscle selection, front/back switching, male/female switching, preference persistence, mobile responsiveness, iPhone Safari (Chromium here, mark SIM CHECK PENDING), reduced motion. Screenshots on small and large phone viewports. Run the existing suite and add tests for the new interactions. ux-critic target 8/10 or higher, as a design review, not a substitute for functional tests.

8. Deliverables in the session report: chosen library and why, why the others were rejected, verified license and asset provenance, what changed, screenshots of all four views, test results and remaining limitations. Prioritize visual quality, anatomical accuracy, male/female parity, mobile performance and reliability over complexity. Do not declare completion until screenshots and functional tests are reviewed.

## Library research (cloud run Nov 1, about 20 minutes, decided)

All six repos exist and are non empty (cloned at depth 1 on Oct 31 UTC).

| Library | License | Female | Art provenance | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| js-rich-body-highlighter (crmapache, 0.1.1) | MIT | yes | Raster body PNGs (1365 x 2048) with masks; no statement of who drew them or how; rendered 3D look | Rejected: the artwork's rights and origin cannot be verified, it may be generated imagery, and four large rasters per theme are heavy for the PWA |
| react-muscle-highlighter (soroojshehryar, 1.2.0) | MIT, but no copyright line | yes | Its four asset files are byte identical to react-native-body-highlighter's, and the original author's copyright notice is dropped | Rejected: a copy without the required MIT notice; use the original instead |
| @emmorts/body-muscles (2.1.1) | Apache 2.0 (fork of vulovix/body-muscles) | no | original | Rejected: male only; female parity is mandatory |
| MuscleMap (Jsplice, 0.1.0) | MIT | yes | Its ASSET_PROVENANCE.md says the muscle paths were traced over OpenAI generated body photos | Rejected: brief forbids AI generated anatomy |
| Sasha's Body Map (Olkre) | none | yes | unknown | Rejected: no license, inspiration only |
| react-native-body-highlighter (HichamELBSI, 3.2.0) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 ELABBASSI Hicham | yes, its own female drawings | Vector path data authored in the repo, shipped under its MIT license | CHOSEN |

Why react-native-body-highlighter: the only candidate with clean, verifiable rights for both code and art, real separate male and female drawings (front and back), individual muscle paths with left and right sides, pure vector (sharp at 320 px, no images to download, works offline). We port only its path data into our own React SVG component (the RN component itself is not used), keep its license in third_party/, and style it with AnyGym tokens.
