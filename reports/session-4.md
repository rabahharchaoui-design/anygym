- 4.4d (Nov 21, live in 7d61d5fc; 4.6b rode along): the run lag fix, first round. What I measured before fixing (simulated iPhone traces, not your phone): the main cause was that every position worse than 25 m accuracy was thrown away, so with a poor GPS start the screen stood still for 91 to 105 s and a walk at 30 m accuracy lost two thirds of its distance; a walk at 15 m accuracy was read as standing for 30 to 37 s at a time; pace took 15 to 36 s to follow a change. Now: positions up to 65 m are kept and weighted by their accuracy, one wild position never adds distance, the phone's own speed decides standing, moving and pace, and with a poor GPS start the distance moves 3 to 7 s after GO. With good GPS the shown distance trails you by about a second and pace follows a change in 5 to 6 s. A new hidden log keeps every raw position of your last 5 runs on the phone (never in a backup, never sent anywhere) so you can export it. NOT DONE, plainly: 21 of 30 first group test cases pass (3 before); in several of the rest the simulated GPS drift alone already misses the 3 percent distance rule. The fix leans on the phone's speed: if your phone gives a noisy or late speed, standing still can still add distance (29 m in 5 minutes in my worst simulation), and inside a long poor GPS stretch a walk can still freeze 18 to 54 s. Only your export can tell which of these your phone is; that is item 4.4e. An independent tester found a real bug in my first version (after a reload mid run, new positions were dropped) and that the start had got slower; both were fixed before this went out. One old test fails on the small Android profile (countdown digits are 49 percent of the screen height, the test wants 50): it is not from this change and goes to M2.
