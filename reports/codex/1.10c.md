1. **Finish can miss an Undo write.** [deleteUndo.ts:66](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/deleteUndo.ts:66) waits on one snapshot of pending writes. While Finish waits for a slow delete, Undo remains available and can enqueue a restore outside that snapshot. Finish can discard the empty session before restoration, leaving an orphaned set. **Fix:** block new delete/undo actions once finishing starts, and drain pending writes until none remain before calling `finishWorkout`.

2. **A failed Undo permanently loses its recovery record.** [deleteUndo.ts:51](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/deleteUndo.ts:51) removes the record before restoration succeeds; [WorkoutScreen.tsx:252](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/screens/workout/WorkoutScreen.tsx:252) says “Try again” without providing an action. **Fix:** retain the deleted record until restore succeeds and supply a retry action that restores that exact record.

3. **Immediate deletion allows duplicate set orders.** [WorkoutScreen.tsx:257](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/screens/workout/WorkoutScreen.tsx:257) now deletes immediately, but `logSet` allocates order from the highest remaining set. Delete the last set, log another, then Undo: both sets receive the deleted set’s order, making History and progression ordering ambiguous. **Fix:** allocate orders from a transactionally maintained, monotonic per-session counter; restoring a set must preserve its original order without reusing it.
