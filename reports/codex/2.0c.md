1. **Pending picker changes are lost when swiping down during a bar save.** [WeightSheet.tsx:35](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/screens/workout/WeightSheet.tsx:35): while `settling`, confirmation returns false, so `onDragClose` closes the sheet and discards the selected plates. The bar choice can still commit afterward. **Fix:** prevent drag dismissal while settling, or defer dismissal and confirmation until the choice completes, using the latest plates.

2. **Changing gym units silently discards an import weight edit.** [ImportSetSheet.tsx:43](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/screens/settings/ImportSetSheet.tsx:43): edit a weight, then change gym units in another tab. `pickable` becomes false, making `changedLoad` false; Save omits the replacement and falls back to the source weight. An invalid edited weight also becomes saveable this way. **Fix:** derive `changedLoad` from `touched` independently of current pickability; preserve the edit’s originating unit and block Save until the touched weight is valid or explicitly reverted.

3. **Failed edits disappear after an app restart, allowing Finish to bypass them.** [logQueue.ts:135](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/logQueue.ts:135): failed edits and set-type changes exist only as callbacks in an in-memory map. Reloading loses both their values and the Finish blocker, contrary to SPEC 13.3’s exact restoration requirement. **Fix:** persist mutation intents and their replacement values in IndexedDB before attempting the write; recover them on startup and remove them only after success or explicit discard.
