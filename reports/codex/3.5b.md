1. **Heavy lifts lose required rest inside circuits.** [quickStyles.ts:336](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/quickStyles.ts:336)  
   At a full gym, the 15-minute Full body circuit includes leg press with 60-second rest. SPEC 22.5–6 requires the heaviest compound to remain unpaired with full rest. **Fix:** Keep heavy compounds outside circuit groups, preserve their prescribed rest during fitting, and reject a card if it cannot fit afterward.

2. **The diversity calculation permits more than half a card’s muscles to overlap.** [quickStyles.ts:514](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/quickStyles.ts:514)  
   At 30 minutes, Superset pump and Arms and abs share biceps and triceps—2 of the latter’s 3 primary muscles. Dividing by the union incorrectly accepts this. **Fix:** Require `2 * shared <= Math.min(a.size, b.size)` and update the property tests accordingly.

3. **Grouped time estimates disagree with the workout’s rest timers.** [quickStyles.ts:45](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/quickStyles.ts:45), [planWorkout.ts:190](/Users/rabah/projects/anygym/src/lib/planWorkout.ts:190)  
   The estimator charges rest once per round, but logging starts the full rest timer after every member. Following those timers makes the advertised circuit/superset duration substantially longer. **Fix:** For generated Quick groups, return zero rest while another member remains behind in the current round; start the group’s rest after the round completes. Test this through the logging path against the estimator.
